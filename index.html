Trading Probability Guide
Even / Odd
10 digits, 5 even, 5 odd. Fair value is 50 / 50. A window reading above 55% on one side is a genuine lean, not noise.

Over / Under
Over 5 wins on 6–9 (40% fair). Under 5 wins on 0–4 (50% fair). Over needs a hotter reading than Under to be worth taking.

Match / Differ
Match is the next digit repeating the previous one — 10% fair. Differ is 90% fair. Anything under 87% differ is a weak edge.

Rise / Fall
Direction of the tick, not the digit. Long streaks are common; treat 55% as the entry threshold.

Digit clustering
Two or three digits absorbing most of the window is the strongest setup. Combine it with the matching side — hot digits in 6–9 means Over, hot odd digits means Odd.

Streaks
Streaks show how long the current run has held. A run of 5+ on one side usually marks the tail of a move; a fresh break out of a run is often the better entry.
